Receiptly.ai

Scan receipts. Find anything.

Public beta on TestFlight, iPhone only

Photograph a receipt and Receiptly reads the shop, the date, the total and every line item, right on your iPhone, in a couple of seconds. Later you search for the thing you bought, not just the shop: type "milk" and you get the line off the receipt with the price you paid.

Version 1.0 has no account and no server. Receipts are read and stored on the phone, in an encrypted database kept out of iCloud backups. There's no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking, so there's nowhere for your data to go.

  • Native iPhone app in Swift and SwiftUI. Apple's Vision framework reads the text, and on newer iPhones Apple's on-device model helps make sense of it.
  • Honest about uncertainty. Every field gets a confidence score, an arithmetic check flags receipts that don't add up, and a field it can't read stays empty rather than guessed.
  • Tested before every release against 220 sample receipts, with minimum accuracy thresholds for fields and for line items.
  • Search by meaning as well as by shop, date, amount or the item code printed on the receipt.

AI Maturity Assessment

AI governance reviews, mapped to the NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001 and the EU AI Act.

An assessor picks an AI vendor or an AI product, works through a guided questionnaire, and gets a maturity report scored across eight governance domains, with the gaps and a remediation plan. An agent runs the session: it chooses the next question, can draft answers from uploaded evidence, scores each response and writes the executive summary.

A walkthrough is available on request: email me.

  • Two kinds of review. A 38-question vendor assessment, and a product assessment that grows from 17 to 71 questions with the product's risk tier.
  • Answers drafted from evidence. Upload up to 30 documents and the model drafts an answer for every applicable question, for the assessor to check.
  • Runs on a local open model (Qwen 2.5 through Ollama), or Claude when configured, with streaming and retries.
  • Built for review and audit: admin, assessor, auditor and viewer roles, short-lived sign-in tokens, rate limits and an audit log.
  • Next.js and SQLite in Docker behind Caddy with HTTPS on AWS, tested with Vitest and Playwright.

Stock Screener

Four stock screens in your inbox before the market opens. Free, and anyone can join.

Every weekday from 7:00 Eastern, four pipelines scan the S&P 1500 and email what they find. Each screen is a fixed set of steps that runs the same way every morning, and the rules are published so readers can judge the output for themselves.

  • Daily digest. Today's earnings reporters, scored 0 to 100 on news and social chatter plus revenue and EPS growth, with a short AI-written thesis for the top names.
  • Unusual volume. Small and mid caps trading at 1.5 times their usual volume or more, and closing up.
  • Accelerating revenue. Companies whose quarter-over-quarter revenue growth has risen four quarters in a row.
  • Momentum. The S&P 500 ranked on several trend measures at once, so no single number dominates.
